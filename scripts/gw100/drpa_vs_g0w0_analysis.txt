VERDICT: all three have the SAME ~0.60 eV scatter once bias is removed. They are

  ΔRPA is nearly unbiased (+0.12) because a total-energy DIFFERENCE cancels the
  correlation error common to cation and neutral.
So ΔRPA "wins" purely by sitting between the two G0W0 biases — a bias-cancellation
effect, NOT superior physics. Confirmed against a CLEAN external @PBE (r=0.72),
so it is not an artifact of ferric's earlier (Padé-node-broken) @PBE.

--- Is ΔRPA "just ΔSCF"? (n=90) — NO, it does TWO things ---
  method    MAE     bias    de-biased RMS
  ΔSCF     1.088   -0.991     0.789
  ΔRPA     0.379   +0.141     0.572
  G0W0@HF  0.493   +0.378     0.571
ΔRPA = ΔSCF + RPA(cation-neutral correlation diff). vs plain ΔSCF it (1) removes
the -0.99 bias (HF has no correlation) AND (2) cuts de-biased scatter 0.79->0.57
(=G0W0 level). So NOT just a bias fix on ΔSCF — the correlation does real
per-molecule work. Known ACFDT relative-energy strength applied to N/N-1.
But de-biased scatter = G0W0's (0.57) and r(ΔRPA,G0W0@HF)=0.97 → NOT better
physics than GW; it just avoids both GW starting-point biases.
