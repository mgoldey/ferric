## Verdict: is this enough to prove out ferric?

**For "the GW machinery is correct" — YES, already proven.**
- ferric G0W0 ≡ PySCF gw_ac to <3 meV (memory: gw-n2-overcorrection).
- ferric G0W0@HF ≡ MOLGW to 5 meV (memory: ferric-gw-spike-results).
- The 0.48 eV MAE is the *correct* answer for evGW@HF on this set — the error
  is in the starting point (HF), not in ferric's implementation. A code that
  got 0.2 eV here would be wrong (lucky cancellation), not better.

**For "ferric is a trustworthy production GW package" — NOT YET. Three gaps:**

1. **Sample size & coverage.** 18 of GW100's 100 molecules, all 1st/2nd-row.
   The published GW100 (van Setten 2015) spans through Xe/I. We cannot claim
   "GW100" — we have a *first/second-row subset*. Heavier atoms (Br, Ge, I, and
   3rd-row metals) will stress ferric's SCF and relativistic gaps.

2. **Only one starting point.** Every number is @HF. The overshoot we measured
   is the single biggest, most reviewer-obvious weakness, and the fix already
   exists unmerged: `feat/gw-pbe-driver` (validated GW@PBE, <0.1 eV vs PySCF).
   @PBE undershoots where @HF overshoots — reporting BOTH starting points (and
   the bracket they form) is what turns this from "a GW calc" into "a
   characterized GW capability."

3. ~~No reference-data cross-check for the new 8 molecules.~~ **Largely CLOSED
   by the literature anchor above.** All 18 (incl. batch-2) now check against
   published CCSD(T) + GW@HF, and the hand-entered exp refs match the GW100
   database to 0.006 eV mean. Remaining: a same-geometry same-basis PySCF
   per-molecule cross-check would make it bit-level (the lit anchor is
   geometry/basis-approximate), but the method is no longer "unverified."

## Speed inefficiencies — DIAGNOSED (two read-only subagents, 2026-06-17)

The aTZ-18 run took ~2h45m niced. Two candidates investigated; verified against
the driver source.

### Finding 1 — within a GW solve: NO redundancy (already optimal). ✓
PDEP/inverse-dielectric intermediates (`inv_dielectric_freq`, `m_proj`) are
built ONCE per `run_gw` and reused across all orbitals and frequencies
(lib.rs:210; sigma.rs:206–213 G0W0; 293–298 evGW₀). No per-orbital rebuild, no
large per-orbital allocations. evGW rebuilds W per outer iteration
(sigma.rs:421–426) — that is the *method definition* (W self-consistency), not
waste. Nothing to fix here.

### Finding 2 — across the 4 GW columns: ~3–4× redundant setup. ✗ (real)
VERIFIED in gw100_full.rs:190–197: the driver calls `run_gw` **4 times per
molecule** (G0W0, COHSEX, evGW₀, evGW). Each call internally rebuilds, via
`run_pdep_rpa` + `mo_b::build_full_b` (lib.rs:210,213):
- the Coulomb metric V (2-center) + its Cholesky inverse-sqrt,
- the ERI3 three-index tensor (P|μν) — the single most expensive op,
- the MO transform, and the full PDEP eigensolve.

Crucially **G0W0, COHSEX, evGW₀ all use the SAME W₀** (screened interaction from
the same neutral RHF) — so three of the four columns could share ONE PDEP+B
build, and evGW reuses it as its iteration-0. Current code throws that away and
rebuilds 4×. The method-specific solvers (`run_g0w0`, `run_cohsex`, …) already
ACCEPT a `PdepRpaResult` by parameter, so the hoist is mechanical.

- **Scope:** this is a BENCHMARK-DRIVER inefficiency (gw100_full.rs), NOT a
  ferric library bug. The library is fine; the driver re-enters the expensive
  path per column.
- **Fix:** refactor the driver to build PDEP+B once per molecule and dispatch
  the 4 methods against the shared intermediates (requires exposing a `run_gw`
  variant that takes pre-built `pdep`/`mo_b`). Est. saving: the GW columns
  collapse from 4 setups to ~1, i.e. roughly halves total GW100 wall time
  (setup-dominated at aTZ). Not done here — it is an API refactor, not a
  one-liner, and should land as its own reviewed change.

### Caveat on the estimate
Agent #2's "60%" figure assumed ERI3 dominates and is unmeasured. Before
committing the refactor, profile ONE molecule to confirm setup (not the
Davidson eigensolve or the evGW outer iterations) is the actual time sink —
otherwise the hoist saves less than advertised. [[ferric-single-job-threading-noop]]
