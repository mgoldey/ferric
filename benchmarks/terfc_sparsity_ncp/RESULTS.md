## Interpretation (separate from the data above)
* Kept amplitudes at relerr 1e-3: NCP/linked = 0.82 (dense C8-C12) and 0.844, 0.843, 0.846, 0.845 (production, C16-C28). No drift with N: a constant ~15-18%
  prefactor, above the ~8% resolution and the 3-6% interpolation bias. At relerr 1e-2: 0.84-0.88 (C8-C16 only).
* Pairs and domain: NCP/linked pairs 0.947, 0.940, 0.938, 0.934 and domain 0.955, 0.949, 0.947, 0.943 at C16-C28, i.e. 5-7% fewer, each point below the 8% resolution:
  suggestive (consistent sign, 4 sizes), not established.
* Tail exponents (kept amplitudes, relerr 1e-3, last 3 points C20-C28; 4-point value in parentheses): Coulomb 1.21 (1.24), erfc 1.15 (1.17), linked 1.15 (1.17), NCP 1.15 (1.17).
  NCP and linked exponents agree to 0.01: the gain is a prefactor, not a scaling change. Pair-count exponents 1.32 / 1.14 / 1.15 / 1.13. These are 3-4 point fits over a 1.4x size range
  (C20-C28); they are measurements, not asymptotic claims, and are well below the earlier pre-onset 3-point values (1.5).
* Cause: the NCP gain is reproduced by a same-range erfc to within 1-3% in keep at 1e-3 (erfc even sparser at 1e-2) despite the different energy scale (0.75 vs 1.25 of Ecoul).
  Consistent with the earlier map: kernel range orders locality. NCP is sparser than linked because its R(0.1) is 1.65 A vs 2.28 A.
* Speed: no win. terfc direct wall is eps-independent (integral cost) and NCP wall = linked wall within 1-7%; terfc is ~5-9x slower than erfc/Coulomb at C28 (1240-1540 s vs 170-260 s, load-confounded).
  A same-range erfc matches NCP's sparsity at a fraction of the wall.

## Verdict (limits)
IMPROVED SPARSITY vs the published linked terfc: demonstrated as a constant ~15-18% reduction in kept amplitudes at relerr 1e-3 over C8-C28 (anchor passing, gate 0.92 met at every size, paired,
larger than the measured scatter of ~0.003 in the ratio), including past the ~C20 onset. NOT demonstrated: a scaling improvement (identical tail exponents), a speed/size win (wall equal to linked, integral-dominated),
or reduced pairs/domains (5-7%, below resolution). The sparsity comes from the shorter kernel range, which a plain erfc reproduces.

## Unverified
* Alkanes only (1-D, 6-31G, no diffuse functions), not aDZ/aTZ where the MP2-V fit was done; no 3-D or polar systems.
* relerr is relative to each operator's own E(0); NCP E(0) is 25% above Coulomb MP2 (sharp-seam overshoot). Sparsity at fixed absolute error vs Coulomb MP2 is not measured.
* Relerr 1e-2 not measured past C20 (terfc); no C32+. Tail fits use 3-4 points over C16-C28.
* Wall exponents unreliable (load, different thread counts for refs); no quiet-box rerun; no matched-accuracy wall vs Coulomb under identical conditions.
* eps* from 2 points at C24/C28; confirming runs only at C8-C12.
* Sparsity is not MP2-V accuracy; the NCP optimum is in-sample (8 A24 systems, aDZ).
* Production-map relerr uses the trivial-map ri_mp2 E(0).
