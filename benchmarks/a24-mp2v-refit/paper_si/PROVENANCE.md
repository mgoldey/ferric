# paper_si/a21x12.json

Numeric tables only, extracted (by the coordinator, from the user-supplied PDF
`ct5b00509_si_001.pdf`, the Supporting Information of Goldey, Belzunces &
Head-Gordon, JCTC 11, 4159 (2015)). The PDF is not committed. Treated as
untrusted data: numbers only. Units kcal/mol. 21 systems x 12 stretch factors
(0.9, 1.0, 1.1, ..., 2.0 in that column order; verified against the si.txt
header row). Keys: '2' SCF/aV5Z CP, '3' SCF/aV5Z non-CP, '4'/'5' MP2/CBS CP/non-CP,
'6'/'7' CCSD(T)/CBS CP/non-CP, '17' MP2(terfc,aTZ), '18' MP2-V(terfc,aTZ),
'19' SCS-MP2-V(2terfc,aTZ). System numbering equals the A24 numbering
(1 water-ammonia, 2 water-dimer, ..., 19 methane-dimer, 20 Ar-methane,
21 Ar-ethene). Extraction was not independently re-checked against the PDF
beyond the spot checks quoted in PREREGISTRATION.md.
