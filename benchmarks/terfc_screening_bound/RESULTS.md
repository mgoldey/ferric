`scripts/run.sh` from there (ignored, env-driven test: MODE=unit|validity|count|lmp2). The scripts in `scripts/`
and the raw logs in `logs/` are kept here as the record; all tables are regenerated by `scripts/analyze.py` into
`logs/tables.md`. Nothing here runs by default or is wired into production.
Configuration: 6-31G orbitals, cc-pVDZ-RI aux, n-alkanes (testdata/molecules), terfc table engine, 3 rayon threads,
shared box (walls are noisy, ratios within one process are the usable numbers). tau is an ABSOLUTE bound on the
largest element of a shell block of normalised AO integrals (Hartree).

## Pre-registration (stated before measuring)

* Physics hypothesis: terfc(r)/r is monotone decreasing and Gaussian-small beyond r0 + ~sqrt(ln(1/tau))/omega, so a
  bound N_ab N_cd K(R_gap) lets a large fraction of the triples/quartets that the existing maps (aux 10 Bohr, virt 12,
  ao_tail 1e-3) keep be skipped, and the skippable fraction should GROW with sharper omega and with molecule size.
* Artifact hypothesis: the bound is only geometric double counting of the existing maps. Then I expect (i) nearly
  nothing skippable among map-surviving triples, and (ii) no dependence on omega (a bound that does not see the kernel
  cannot rank operators).
* Discriminator added during the work: Schwarz (kernel-independent) vs Schwarz plus a kernel-aware bound, on the SAME
  map-surviving evaluations; the kernel-specific increment is (S or B1) minus S.

## Bounds implemented (no integrals needed, analytic shell data only)

* Majorant of each shell function: |phi_f(r)| <= sum_i d_i exp(-beta_i |r-A|^2), d_i = sqrt((2l+1)/4pi) N_rad |c_i| m_l,
  beta_i = f*alpha_i (f = 0.9 for l>0, 1 for l=0), m_l = (l/(2e(1-f)alpha))^(l/2). Rigorous for every component.
* B1 (the user's candidate 1): N_ab N_P K(max gap) with N = L1 mass of the majorant, gap = centre distance minus the two
  mass-quantile extents (delta = fraction of majorant mass outside). NOT rigorous beyond delta; validated empirically.
* B2 (candidate 2, rigorous, no extents): att(r) <= exp(-w^2 (r-r0)_+^2) <= C_eps exp(-kappa_eps r^2) with
  C_eps = exp(w^2 r0^2 (1/eps-1)), kappa_eps = w^2 (1-eps), minimised over an eps grid; 1/r = (2/sqrt(pi)) int exp(-t^2 r^2) dt;
  the result is a sum of analytic ssss Gaussian integrals followed by a 1-D t quadrature (+ a rigorous analytic tail
  remainder). Cost per triple is far above a table-engine primitive triple, so B2 is a TIGHTNESS reference, not a
  production candidate. (Quadrature check against closed-form Coulomb ssss: +9e-5 relative, on the safe side.)
* Schwarz: sqrt((P|P)(ab|ab)) with the terfc engine's own diagonals (valid: terfc is a positive-definite kernel).
  `SchwarzBounds::compute` refuses terfc, which is why production forces schwarz_skip = 0 for it; the diagonals are
  trivially available from `compute_quartet(a,b,a,b)` / `compute_eri2(p,p)`.

* Anchor: the bound is vacuous-but-valid when it should be. B1 returns +inf (no claim) whenever the extents overlap
  (67% of C4 triples at delta=1e-2, 100% for water/aug); min(S,B1) is then Schwarz. tau -> 0 keeps everything.
  Schwarz minimum ratio 1.0000 is the diagonal itself (attained).
* The check can fail: halving the B1 extents gives violations for NCP (quartets 43, triples 144 at delta=1e-2) and s=2
  (triples 5252, quartets 6225), and halving B2 gives 71..479 violations in every set. LIMIT: for the linked operator
  (s=0.7071) the halved-extent B1 does NOT violate anywhere (the bound is too loose there to be falsified at this
  size), so for the linked operator the empirical delta-validity rests on the C12/C8 oracle-miss counts, not on a mutation.

  ignores sign cancellation / multipole suppression of l>0 shells), B2 is tight to ~16-60x median and ~1.01-1.06 at the
  minimum, comparable to Schwarz (median 16). Water/aug: B1 never applies (diffuse extents).

## (d) Is the bound tight? Second candidate (Gaussian majorant)

* B1 alone: weaker than Schwarz at C12 (61% vs 53% survivors) and only combines usefully with it (S&B1 42%).
* B2 (rigorous) alone: better than B1 alone (45% vs 61% survivors at C12, 87.6% vs 75.5% skippable at C32) and
  ~equal to Schwarz+B1 at C12 (45.0% vs 42.1%), but below it at C20/C32 (S or B2 skips 78% / 87.7%; S or B1 84% / 93.6%).
  B2 never missed an oracle element (0 misses in 20k samples x 5 operators x 3 tau), so it is the rigorous reference.
* Remaining gap to the oracle (C12 linked tau=1e-6: oracle 26% vs 42% S&B1 vs 45% S&B2) is mostly monopole-only
  majorants (L1 norms ignore sign cancellation / multipole suppression of l>0 functions) rather than kernel geometry:
  evidence = validity median ratios (B1 1e3..1e6, B2 ~16-60) and the large false-positive share. Interpretation, not
  separately tested.

## Verdict

1. YES, a cheap controlled bound exists and works end to end: Schwarz (rigorous) skips 51% / 72% / 83% of the
   map-surviving terfc 3-index evaluations at C12 / C20 / C32 (tau=1e-6) and gives 2.2x faster eri3 at C12 with a
   relative energy change of 8e-11; adding the extent-based distance bound B1 (not rigorous, 0 violations in
   2.35M triples + 4.28M quartets with oracle) raises this to 61% / 84% / 94% and 2.9x (C12) to 3.5x (C16) measured wall.
2. The artifact hypothesis is mostly TRUE for the dominant part and FALSE for the increment. Most of what is removable is
   kernel-INDEPENDENT (Schwarz: pair-overlap decay and the missing primitive screening), the same for Coulomb and
   terfc and identical across omega (S skippable 71.6% for linked and NCP at C20). The existing maps do not capture it
   (they remove none of the unique triples at C12-C32). The kernel-decay part (S-or-B1 minus S) is real, operator
   dependent and ordered by omega: at C12 global tau=1e-6 survivors Schwarz 0.53 -> S&B1 0.52 (Coulomb), 0.46 (erfc 0.42),
   0.42 (linked), 0.36 (NCP), 0.34 (s=2); at C20 skip 84.0% (linked) vs 86.7% (NCP). It is worth ~11-19 points of the skip
   at C12 (global), ~11-15 at C20/C32, and moving linked -> NCP buys ~1.2x less eri3 work; s=2 another ~1.1x.
3. The 1/omega-style tail radius picture is right (table (a)) but is not what limits the gain: B1 needs
   R_gap = R_centres - e_ab - e_P, and with delta=1e-4 the extents are several Bohr for the diffuse valence shells,
   so the effective centre cutoff is ~R(tau) + 6-9 Bohr; the sharper-omega advantage is mostly eaten. For the refit's
   sharper-seam direction (s = 1.354 and above, E_att/E_Coul > 1) the screening gain keeps improving monotonically with s,
   but modestly; it never beats the kernel-independent baseline in size.
4. The "constant 15-18% sparser amplitudes" of NCP and this "~1.2x less 3-index work at fixed tau" are consistent in
   size and have the same sign; neither changes the picture that terfc wall is set by unscreened integrals.

## Unverified / limits

* B1 is a controlled, not rigorous, bound: delta tail mass unaccounted; zero violations observed (C4, C12, C8 sets) but the
  halved-extent mutation does not fire for the linked operator, so for linked the evidence is the oracle-miss counts only.
  B1 is vacuous for diffuse (aug) shells at molecule sizes tested (water) and was not validated on heavier atoms / charged
  or non-alkane geometries. Only alkanes, 6-31G / cc-pVDZ-RI (+ d-shell orbital set on C4, aug-cc-pVDZ on water).
* Applied-energy tests: C12 and C16, linked and NCP only, one eps (5e-5), one set of maps. C20/C32 are count-only.
  The skip fractions at C28 and the walls in (f) are linear extrapolations; applied wall at C28 not run (>20 min per run).
* The unique-triple counter (maps remove none of the unique triples) is untested against a setting where it reads < 100%.
* Wall times are from a shared, loaded box (load 6-10); within one process the ratios are consistent with the work
  fractions, absolute seconds are not.
* tau is absolute per element of normalised-AO blocks; the user-visible error budget (energy) was only measured, not
  propagated. The primitive-level count is a bound count, no kernel was changed, and the claim "no primitive screening
  in compute_cart_eri3" is from reading the loop, not from a counter.
* Quartets: B2 sampled only (3000-3500 per case); no applied-energy test (the pipeline does not use quartets).
* Fair erfc/Coulomb comparison with their production schwarz_skip = 1e-5 not measured.
