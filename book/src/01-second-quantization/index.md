# Second quantization {#second-quantization}

Every method in this book works with the same electronic Hamiltonian. This
chapter writes it in the language the derivations use, second quantization,
where the antisymmetry of the electrons lives in the operators instead of
in the wavefunction. It covers three steps:

- The Hamiltonian in spin orbitals and in spatial orbitals, the energy of
  one determinant, and the split of the Hamiltonian into a mean field and
  a fluctuation (@sec:hamiltonian).
- Wick's theorem, and how the proof scripts use it to derive working
  equations and their prefactors without hand bookkeeping (@sec:wick).
- The reduction from spin orbitals to spatial orbitals, which is where the
  closed-shell and unrestricted working equations in later chapters come
  from (@sec:spin-adaptation).
