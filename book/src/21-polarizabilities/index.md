# 21. Polarizabilities, charges and export

The response of a molecule to a uniform field, computed from the dielectric
eigenmodes of chapter 18, and what the response and the density become as
numbers handed to other tools. Section 21.1 is the polarizability, section
21.2 the atomic partitions that turn a density or a response into per-atom
numbers, and section 21.3 the schemas the numbers are written in.
