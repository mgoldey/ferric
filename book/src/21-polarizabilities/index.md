# Polarizabilities, charges and export {#polarizabilities}

A molecule in a uniform field polarizes, and how much it polarizes at
imaginary frequency sets its dispersion coefficients. This chapter computes
that response from the dielectric eigenmodes of chapter @sec:rpa, splits it and the
density among the atoms, and writes the results as arrays for other tools.
Section @sec:polarizabilities-from-dielectric-modes is the molecular polarizability, static and dynamic. Section
@sec:atomic-partitions is the atomic partitions that turn a density or a response into per-atom
numbers: charges, atomic polarizabilities and the charge-transfer remainder.
Section @sec:export-schemas is the file schemas the numbers are written in.
