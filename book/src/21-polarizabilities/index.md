# 21. Polarizabilities, charges and export

A molecule in a uniform field polarizes, and how much it polarizes at
imaginary frequency sets its dispersion coefficients. This chapter computes
that response from the dielectric eigenmodes of chapter 18, splits it and the
density among the atoms, and writes the results as arrays for other tools.
Section 21.1 is the molecular polarizability, static and dynamic. Section
21.2 is the atomic partitions that turn a density or a response into per-atom
numbers: charges, atomic polarizabilities and the charge-transfer remainder.
Section 21.3 is the file schemas the numbers are written in.
