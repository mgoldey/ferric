# 9. Kohn–Sham density functional theory

The Kohn–Sham energy and Fock matrix, the functional families ferric
evaluates through libxc, the atom-centred quadrature grid, the
exchange-correlation matrix elements, exact exchange in hybrid and
range-separated hybrid functionals and the tuning of $\omega$, VV10 nonlocal
correlation, the second-derivative kernels the response methods use, and
constrained DFT with the diabatic coupling. The XC nuclear gradient is in
the KS gradient sections; the KS Z-vector is section 15.4; TDDFT is
chapter 20.

Every equation of this chapter has its derivation in
`crates/ferric-dft/theory.md`, which the sections include by heading.
