# 20. Time-dependent density functional theory

Linear response of a converged SCF reference: the response matrices
$\mathbf A$ and $\mathbf B$, the Tamm–Dancoff approximation and the Casida
equations with their dense and matrix-free solvers, transition dipoles and
oscillator strengths, the exchange-correlation kernel and the functional
gate, range-separated hybrids, triplets, and the spin-conserving response of
UHF and UKS references with $\langle S^2\rangle$ for each root. The kernel
itself is section 9.6.

Every equation of this chapter has its derivation in
`crates/ferric-tddft/theory.md`, which the sections include by heading.
