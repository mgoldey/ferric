# MP3 {#mp3}

<!-- status: ready; registry: mp3;
     theory: crates/ferric-mp2/theory.md#"6. MP3", "6.1 Spin-blocked unrestricted MP3" -->

MP2 lets a pair of electrons jump into two virtual orbitals and come back,
with one bare interaction each way. Third-order Møller–Plesset theory lets
the excited pair interact once more while it is excited: the two particles
can scatter off each other among the virtuals, the two holes can scatter
among the occupied orbitals, or a particle and a hole can scatter
together, which brings a third occupied orbital into the pair. Those are the three terms of $E^{(3)}$,
and the first of them, the particle–particle ladder, sets the cost:
$O(n_o^2 n_v^4)$, against MP2's $O(n_o^2 n_v^2 N_{\mathrm{aux}})$.

ferric has two implementations: a spin-orbital one behind the CLI and
Python, and a spin-blocked unrestricted one in the library. The plan:
the energy expressions (@sec:mp3-energy), the spin-orbital code (@sec:mp3-spin-orbital-implementation), the
spin-blocked code (@sec:mp3-spin-blocked-unrestricted-mp3), and what is anchored (@sec:mp3-anchored).

## The energy {#mp3-energy}

Work in spin orbitals with the first-order amplitudes of section @sec:closed-shell-ri-mp2,
$t_{ij}^{ab} = -\langle ij\|ab\rangle/\Delta_{ij}^{ab}$. The second-order
energy is the overlap of the amplitudes with the integrals that drive them:

$$ E^{(2)} = \tfrac14 \sum_{ijab} t_{ij}^{ab}\,\langle ij\|ab\rangle , \label{eq:mp3-1} $$

which is (@eq:closed-shell-ri-mp2-1) after substituting $t$. The third-order energy inserts one
more interaction between two first-order amplitudes. Sort the inserted
integral by how many of its indices are occupied:

$$ E^{(3)} = \underbrace{\tfrac18 \sum t_{ij}^{ab}\,\langle ab\|cd\rangle\,t_{ij}^{cd}}_{\text{pp ladder}} + \underbrace{\tfrac18 \sum \langle kl\|ij\rangle\,t_{ij}^{ab}\,t_{kl}^{ab}}_{\text{hh ladder}} + \underbrace{\sum t_{ij}^{ab}\,\langle kb\|cj\rangle\,t_{ik}^{ac}}_{\text{ring}} . \label{eq:mp3-coefficients} $$

The sums run over all spin-orbital indices: $i, j, k, l$ occupied and
$a, b, c, d$ virtual. The integrals are RI-factorized (@eq:closed-shell-ri-mp2-4) and
antisymmetrized; the MP3 energy reported is $E^{(2)} + E^{(3)}$.

Read each term as a process:

- **pp ladder.** $\langle ab\|cd\rangle$ has four virtual indices. The pair
  excited to $cd$ scatters to $ab$; the occupied pair $ij$ is a spectator.
- **hh ladder.** $\langle kl\|ij\rangle$ has four occupied indices. The two
  holes move from $kl$ to $ij$ while the particles $ab$ stay put.
- **ring.** $\langle kb\|cj\rangle$ has two of each. One particle and one
  hole change together: $b \to c$ and $j \to k$.

Count the free indices of each contraction to get its cost: the pp ladder
has $i, j, a, b, c, d$, so $O(n_o^2 n_v^4)$; the hh ladder has
$i, j, k, l, a, b$, so $O(n_o^4 n_v^2)$; the ring has $i, j, k, a, b, c$,
so $O(n_o^3 n_v^3)$. With $n_v > n_o$ in any useful basis the pp ladder
dominates.

Why $\tfrac18$ on the ladders and 1 on the ring? Write $E^{(3)}$ as the
overlap $\tfrac14\sum t_{ij}^{ab} R_{ijab}$ of the amplitudes with the MP3
doubles residual $R$ of section @sec:mp3-spin-blocked-unrestricted-mp3 (substitute $c^{(2)} = -R/\Delta$
and $t = -K/\Delta$ into (@eq:mp3-u-spin-blocks)). The ladder terms of $R$ carry
$\tfrac12$, because the sum over $cd$ (or $kl$) counts each unordered pair
twice; $\tfrac14 \times \tfrac12 = \tfrac18$. The ring term of $R$ carries
the permutation $P(ij)P(ab)$, four terms that give equal contributions
against an amplitude antisymmetric in $ij$ and in $ab$;
$\tfrac14 \times 4 = 1$. In Wick's theorem the same count appears as four
orderings that produce the ring diagram, each contributing $\tfrac14$ of
the contraction as written. One ordering alone, $\tfrac14$, is the wrong
value; the proof below carries it as its negative control.

## Spin-orbital implementation {#mp3-spin-orbital-implementation}

`mp3_energy` builds the RI 3-index MO tensors per spatial block (occupied–
occupied, occupied–virtual, virtual–virtual), expands them to spin orbitals
(spin orbital $2p + \sigma$) and antisymmetrizes. The contraction
`mp3_energy_from_spin_orbital_blocks` then follows (@eq:mp3-1)–(@eq:mp3-coefficients) term by
term. Its three $E^{(3)}$ terms, with the tensor wrapping and the comments
on freeing memory removed:

```rust
// pp ladder: 1/8 t_ijab <ab||cd> t_ijcd
let x = einsum!("ijab,abcd->ijcd", &t_t, &vvvv_t);
0.125 * einsum!("ijcd,ijcd->", &x_t, &t_t)
// hh ladder: 1/8 <kl||ij> t_ijab t_klab
let y = einsum!("klij,ijab->klab", &oooo_t, &t_t);
0.125 * einsum!("klab,klab->", &y_t, &t_t)
// ring: sum t_ijab <kb||cj> t_ikac
let z = einsum!("ijab,kbcj->iakc", &t_t, &ovvo_t);       // z_iakc = sum_jb t_ijab <kb||cj>
let t_iakc = t.permuted_axes(IxDyn(&[0, 2, 1, 3])).to_owned();   // t_ikac stored as (i,a,k,c)
einsum!("iakc,iakc->", &z_t, &t_iakc_t)
```

- **The amplitudes carry the code's sign.** They are built as
  `asym_oovv_t[[i, j, a, b]] / d` with
  $d = \varepsilon_i + \varepsilon_j - \varepsilon_a - \varepsilon_b = -\Delta$,
  which is $-\langle ij\|ab\rangle/\Delta$.
- **Each term is two contractions.** The first builds an intermediate with
  the shape of an amplitude; the second is a full overlap with $t$.
- **The ring needs a transpose.** $t_{ik}^{ac}$ must be laid out as
  $(i, a, k, c)$ to match `z`, and the permuted copy is the last use of $t$.

Every contraction runs through the `einsum!` macro. The spin-orbital
virtual–virtual–virtual–virtual block $\langle ab\|cd\rangle$ has
$(2n_v)^4 = 16\,n_v^4$ elements. That block, not the arithmetic, limits the
system size. In 8-byte doubles it is 17 MB for water in cc-pVDZ
($n_v = 19$) and 9.6 GB for benzene in cc-pVDZ ($n_v = 93$). The call is pre-flighted against the
memory budget and refuses a run whose peak does not fit.

## Spin-blocked unrestricted MP3 {#mp3-spin-blocked-unrestricted-mp3}

Most of the $16\,n_v^4$ elements are zero by spin. `u_mp3` keeps only the
blocks spin allows, as UMP2 does (section @sec:unrestricted-ri-mp2): $\alpha\alpha$,
$\beta\beta$ and $\alpha\beta$, the last stored with $\alpha$ first,
$c^{\alpha\beta}_{iJaB}$ (lower case $\alpha$, upper case $\beta$). With $K$
the antisymmetrized ($\alpha\alpha$, $\beta\beta$) or plain ($\alpha\beta$)
integrals,

$$ K^{\sigma\sigma}_{ijab} = (ia|jb) - (ib|ja), \qquad K^{\alpha\beta}_{iJaB} = (ia|JB), $$

the two amplitude orders are

$$ c^{(1)} = -K/\Delta, \qquad c^{(2)} = -R\big[c^{(1)}\big]/\Delta , $$

with $R$ the MP3 doubles residual (the spin-orbital
$R_{ijab} = \tfrac12\sum\langle ab\|cd\rangle t_{ijcd} + \tfrac12\sum\langle kl\|ij\rangle t_{klab} + P(ij)P(ab)\sum\langle kb\|cj\rangle t_{ikac}$,
split block by block in `crates/ferric-mp2/theory.md` §6.1). The energy of
order $n = 2, 3$ is the overlap of the previous-order amplitudes with $K$:

$$ E^{(n)} = \tfrac14 \sum c^{(n-1)}_{\alpha\alpha} K_{\alpha\alpha} + \tfrac14 \sum c^{(n-1)}_{\beta\beta} K_{\beta\beta} + \sum c^{(n-1)}_{\alpha\beta} K_{\alpha\beta} . \label{eq:mp3-u-spin-blocks} $$

Why $\tfrac14$ on the same-spin blocks and 1 on $\alpha\beta$? The
same-spin blocks are antisymmetric in $ij$ and in $ab$, so each pair
appears four times in the sum, as in (@eq:mp3-1). The $\alpha\beta$ block
stores each pair once, and the four spin-orbital orderings that map onto
it ($i\bar J a\bar B$, $\bar J i a\bar B$, $i\bar J\bar B a$,
$\bar J i\bar B a$) cancel the $\tfrac14$.

Check the closed-shell limit. For an RHF input the blocks satisfy
$c^{\alpha\alpha}_{ijab} = c^{\alpha\beta}_{ijab} - c^{\alpha\beta}_{ijba}$
and $c^{\beta\beta} = c^{\alpha\alpha}$ at both orders, and the energies
equal those of `mp3.rs`.

The code is (@eq:mp3-u-spin-blocks) word for word. This is `channel_energy` in
`ferric-mp2/src/u_mp3.rs`:

```rust
UMp3Channels {
    aa: 0.25 * contract(&c.aa, &k.aa),   // 1/4 sum c_aa K_aa
    bb: 0.25 * contract(&c.bb, &k.bb),   // 1/4 sum c_bb K_bb
    ab: contract(&c.ab, &k.ab),          // sum c_ab K_ab
}
```

The energies come out per spin channel: the $\alpha\alpha + \beta\beta$ part
of $E^{(2)}$ is the same-spin energy and the $\alpha\beta$ part the
opposite-spin energy, and $E^{(3)}$ splits the same way. So SCS weights can
be applied to $E^{(3)}$ as well as $E^{(2)}$, and section @sec:opmp3 builds on
these channels.

| Property | Spin orbital (`mp3.rs`) | Spin blocked (`u_mp3.rs`) |
|---|---|---|
| References | RHF | RHF, UHF, semicanonical ROHF (doubles only, no singles) |
| Largest virtual block | $16\,n_v^4$ | $\max(n_v^\alpha, n_v^\beta)^4$ (exact integrals); one $n_v^3$ slab at a time (RI) |
| Integrals | RI | RI or exact four-centre |
| Amplitudes returned | no | $K$, $c^{(1)}$, $c^{(2)}$ |
| Reached from | CLI, Python, Rust | Rust only |

## Known limits (2026-11-15)

- CLI and Python MP3 are closed-shell (RHF) and spin-orbital; the
  open-shell `u_mp3` is library-only.
- No MP3 gradient; no orbital-optimized MP3.
- The ROHF reference omits the singles contributions.
