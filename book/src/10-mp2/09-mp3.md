# 10.9 MP3

Third-order Møller–Plesset theory adds one more order of the fluctuation
potential to MP2. Its cost is set by the particle–particle ladder,
$O(n_o^2 n_v^4)$, against MP2's $O(n_o^2 n_v^2 N_{\mathrm{aux}})$. ferric has
two implementations: a spin-orbital one behind the CLI and Python, and a
spin-blocked unrestricted one in the library.

| Entry point | Location |
|---|---|
| Rust (spin orbital, RHF) | `ferric-mp2/src/mp3.rs::mp3_energy`, contraction in `ferric-mp2/src/mp3.rs::mp3_energy_from_spin_orbital_blocks` |
| Rust (spin blocked: RHF, UHF, ROHF) | `ferric-mp2/src/u_mp3.rs::u_mp3` |
| CLI | `method.kind = "mp3"`, `[mp2] auxbasis`, `frozen_core` |
| Python | `run_mp3` |

## 10.9.1 The energy

In spin orbitals with first-order amplitudes
$t_{ij}^{ab} = -\langle ij\|ab\rangle/\Delta_{ij}^{ab}$,

$$ E^{(2)} = \tfrac14 \sum_{ijab} t_{ij}^{ab}\,\langle ij\|ab\rangle , \tag{10.22} $$

$$ E^{(3)} = \underbrace{\tfrac18 \sum t_{ij}^{ab}\,\langle ab\|cd\rangle\,t_{ij}^{cd}}_{\text{pp ladder}} + \underbrace{\tfrac18 \sum \langle kl\|ij\rangle\,t_{ij}^{ab}\,t_{kl}^{ab}}_{\text{hh ladder}} + \underbrace{\sum t_{ij}^{ab}\,\langle kb\|cj\rangle\,t_{ik}^{ac}}_{\text{ring}} . \tag{10.23} $$

The sums run over all spin-orbital indices: $i, j, k, l$ occupied and
$a, b, c, d$ virtual. The integrals are RI-factorized (10.4) and
antisymmetrized; the MP3 energy reported is $E^{(2)} + E^{(3)}$.

The ring coefficient is 1. Each of the four Wick orderings that produce the
ring diagram contributes $\tfrac14$ of the contraction as written, and the
four together give coefficient 1. One ordering alone, $\tfrac14$, is the
wrong value.

## 10.9.2 Spin-orbital implementation

`mp3_energy` builds the RI 3-index MO tensors per spatial block (occupied–
occupied, occupied–virtual, virtual–virtual), expands them to spin orbitals
and antisymmetrizes. Every contraction runs through the `einsum!` macro.

The spin-orbital virtual–virtual–virtual–virtual block
$\langle ab\|cd\rangle$ has $(2n_v)^4 = 16\,n_v^4$ elements. That block, not
the arithmetic, limits the system size: the call is pre-flighted against the
memory budget and refuses a run whose peak does not fit.

## 10.9.3 Spin-blocked unrestricted MP3

`u_mp3` holds amplitudes in three spin blocks, as UMP2 does (section 10.2):
$\alpha\alpha$, $\beta\beta$, $\alpha\beta$. With $K$ the antisymmetrized
($\alpha\alpha$, $\beta\beta$) or plain ($\alpha\beta$) integrals,

$$ c^{(1)} = -K/\Delta, \qquad c^{(2)} = -R\big[c^{(1)}\big]/\Delta , $$

$$ E^{(n)} = \tfrac14 \sum c^{(n-1)}_{\alpha\alpha} K_{\alpha\alpha} + \tfrac14 \sum c^{(n-1)}_{\beta\beta} K_{\beta\beta} + \sum c^{(n-1)}_{\alpha\beta} K_{\alpha\beta} , \tag{10.24} $$

with $R$ the MP3 doubles residual and $n = 2, 3$. The energies come out per
spin channel, so SCS weights can be applied to $E^{(3)}$ as well as
$E^{(2)}$.

| Property | Spin orbital (`mp3.rs`) | Spin blocked (`u_mp3.rs`) |
|---|---|---|
| References | RHF | RHF, UHF, semicanonical ROHF (doubles only, no singles) |
| Largest virtual block | $16\,n_v^4$ | $\max(n_v^\alpha, n_v^\beta)^4$ (exact integrals); one $n_v^3$ slab at a time (RI) |
| Integrals | RI | RI or exact four-centre |
| Amplitudes returned | no | $K$, $c^{(1)}$, $c^{(2)}$ |
| Reached from | CLI, Python, Rust | Rust only |

## Known limits (2026-11-15)

- CLI and Python MP3 are closed-shell (RHF) and spin-orbital; the
  open-shell `u_mp3` is library-only.
- No MP3 gradient; no orbital-optimized MP3.
- The ROHF reference omits the singles contributions.
